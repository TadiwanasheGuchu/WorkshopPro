# Mechanics and technicians

Designed for your phone. Open WorkshopPro in the phone's browser and add it to the home
screen for quick access.

## Your jobs

The dashboard shows **My open jobs** and **Overdue**.

**Job cards → Assigned to me** lists the open jobs you are assigned to, most urgent first.
Open a job to see the complaint, requested work, vehicle details, notes, the approved
quotation and the parts issued.

The buttons at the bottom of the job show only the next steps you are allowed to take,
for example **Start diagnosis**, **Start repair**, **Send to quality check**.
If **Start repair** is missing, usually the customer has not approved the work yet — check with
reception or the manager. Do not start chargeable work without approval.

## Inspections

On the job card, **Start inspection** and choose the checklist (for example *Intake
inspection* or *Quality inspection*).

For each item choose **Pass**, **Attention required**, **Failed** or **Not inspected**,
add a short note where useful, and tap **Photo** to take a picture. Answers save as you
go; wait for **Saved** before leaving the page, especially on a weak signal.

**Submit inspection** when finished. Every item marked *Attention required* or *Failed*
needs a note or a photo. After submission the inspection is locked; corrections are made
with **Amend submitted inspection** and a reason, and both versions are kept. **Print view** gives the
customer-readable report.

Photos: hold the phone steady, fill the frame with the item, and take a second photo from
further back if location matters. Photos are resized automatically and location data is
removed.

## Diagnosis and work record

Use **Edit job details** to record the **diagnosis** and, as you work, the **work
performed**. Add notes for anything the next person needs to know. Write plainly: the
customer may read the work record on their invoice and in the portal.

## Parts

Parts are reserved for the job when the quotation is approved and **issued** to the job
when you actually take them from stores. If a part is missing or wrong, tell the parts
person or manager; they can reserve, issue or **Return** parts on the job card.

## Finishing

When the work is done, **Send to quality check**. The manager or a senior mechanic
completes the quality inspection and marks the vehicle **Ready for collection**.
