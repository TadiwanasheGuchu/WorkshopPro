# Reception and service advisors

You register customers and vehicles, book work, receive vehicles, send quotations, keep
the customer informed and hand vehicles back.

## Customers

**Customers → Register customer.** Choose *Individual* or *Company*. Add every phone
number and email you have; mark the main one. Record how the customer wants to be
contacted and their **consent** to messages (email, WhatsApp). Without consent the system
does not send them messages.

Before registering, search first (name, phone number or registration) to avoid
duplicates. A customer you no longer serve can be archived; their history is kept.

The customer page shows their vehicles, jobs, quotations, invoices, **balance owed**, and
**Messages sent to this customer**.

## Vehicles

**Vehicles → Register vehicle**, or **Add vehicle** on the customer's page. Registration
or VIN is required; everything else is optional but helps (make, model, year, engine
number, colour, odometer). If a vehicle changes hands, use **Transfer owner** on the
vehicle page; history stays with the vehicle.

Set **next service due** on the vehicle to have the system send a service reminder.

## Appointments and booking requests

**Appointments** shows one day per branch. **Book appointment** to reserve a time, bay and
mechanic; the system refuses double bookings.

Customers can also request a booking from the customer portal. Requests appear on the
Appointments page: **Confirm** (choose the actual date and time; the customer is told)
or **Decline** with a reason.

When the customer arrives, **Check in** on the appointment opens a job card.

## Receiving a vehicle

**Job cards → Receive vehicle** (or **Receive vehicle** on the vehicle page):

1. Choose the customer and vehicle.
2. Record the **customer complaint** in their words and the **requested work**.
3. Record **odometer in** and **fuel in**, and the **promised** date/time if agreed.
4. **Open job card.** The job gets its number (for example JC-000123) and the customer
   gets a "vehicle received" message if they agreed to messages.

Walk round the vehicle with the customer and note existing damage in the intake
inspection (see [Mechanics](mechanics.md)) — photos protect the workshop and the customer.

## Quotations and approval

After diagnosis, on the job card: **Prepare quotation**. Add labour, parts and fees;
totals and tax are calculated by the system. **Save draft** while working.

To get approval:

- **Send to customer** creates a secure link (and emails/WhatsApps it if the customer
  agreed to messages). The customer sees the exact quotation and approves or rejects it.
  Opening the link is never treated as approval.
- If the customer decides in person, by phone or by email, use **Record customer
  decision**, choose how they told you, and add their name and any evidence.

If the price or work changes, **Revise quotation**: this creates a new version and the
customer must approve again. Quotations show **Valid until**; expired quotations cannot be
approved.

## Keeping the customer informed

Messages are sent automatically at key points (vehicle received, quotation sent, ready for
collection, invoice issued, booking confirmed, service due) by the customer's preferred
channel, if they consented. **Notifications** shows every message and whether it was
delivered or failed and why. Messages never contain passwords.

## Customer portal

On the customer page, **Give portal access** with the customer's email. They receive a
sign-in link (no password) and can see their vehicles, job progress, quotations
(approve/reject), invoices and receipts, and request bookings. **Remove access** at any
time; **Send a new sign-in link** if theirs expired.

## Handing over

When the job is **Ready for collection** and the invoice is settled (or the manager agrees
otherwise), open the job card and **Hand over vehicle**: record who collected it and any
handover notes. The job becomes **Completed / collected** and the vehicle's service
history is updated.
