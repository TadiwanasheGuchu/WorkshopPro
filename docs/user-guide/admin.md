# Administration (owner)

## Users and roles

**Users & roles → Invite user**: name, email and one or more roles per branch. The person
receives an invitation link that expires; **Resend invitation** if needed. **Deactivate
user** when someone leaves — this signs them out everywhere immediately. Their past
actions stay in the records.

Roles start from templates (Owner, Workshop manager, Service advisor, Mechanic, Inventory
manager, Accounts) and can be adjusted or copied with **New role**. Give each person the
least access they need. Changes to roles and permissions are audited.

## Branches

**Branches → Add branch**: name, code, address, operating hours and **service bays**
(**Add bay**). Archive a branch that closes; its history remains.

## Settings

- **Organisation details**: trading and legal name, registration and tax numbers,
  address and contact details. These appear on quotations, invoices and receipts.
- **Preferences**: currency (NAD), time zone (Africa/Windhoek), date format, whether
  prices include tax, quotation validity and terms, invoice payment days and terms,
  document footer, service reminder window.
- **Tax rates**: none are pre-loaded. Add the rates and wording your accountant or tax
  adviser confirms (**Add tax rate**). Do not guess.
- **Document numbering**: prefixes and padding for job cards, quotations, invoices,
  credit notes, receipts, purchase orders and deliveries. Numbers never repeat.
- **Inspection checklists**: edit the default intake and quality checklists or create new
  ones with **New checklist**. Submitted inspections keep the checklist as it was.

## Messages to customers

**Notifications → Message templates**: wording for each automatic message. WhatsApp
messages must match templates approved by Meta for the workshop's WhatsApp Business
account; until that account is set up, WhatsApp messages are logged but not sent.

## Audit log

**Audit logs** shows who did what and when: sign-ins, permission changes, approvals and
exceptions, invoices, payments, stock adjustments, status changes. Filter by action, record
or person. The log cannot be edited.

## Backups and support

Backups, updates and monitoring are handled under the maintenance agreement (see the
installation record for contacts). Report problems with the time, the screen, what you
did, and the request ID if an error message shows one.
