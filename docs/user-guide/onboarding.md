# Onboarding plan for a pilot workshop

A two-week plan to take a workshop from installation to running real jobs, with a clear
fallback to the existing process. Adjust dates to the workshop.

## Before training (installer + owner)

- [ ] Server installed and handed over ([installation runbook](../deployment/installation.md)).
- [ ] Organisation details, branches, bays, operating hours entered.
- [ ] Tax rates and invoice wording confirmed by the workshop's accountant or tax adviser
      and entered.
- [ ] Document numbering agreed (continue from the workshop's current invoice numbers if
      the accountant requires it).
- [ ] Staff list with roles; invitations sent; everyone has signed in once.
- [ ] Parts list imported or the 50 most-used parts entered with opening stock (stocktake
      adjustment).
- [ ] Message consent approach agreed (ask each customer at the counter; record it).
- [ ] Phones/tablets for mechanics checked: browser up to date, home-screen shortcut,
      camera permission allowed.

## Training sessions (45 minutes each, on the real system)

Use a **training customer and vehicle** (clearly named, for example "TRAINING — do not
invoice") and archive them afterwards.

| Session | Who | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Everyone | Signing in, menu, job statuses, approval rule, where to get help ([Basics](README.md)) |
| 2 | Reception | Customer and vehicle registration, receiving a vehicle, quotations and approval, handover ([Reception](reception.md)) |
| 3 | Mechanics | Assigned jobs on the phone, intake inspection with photos, status moves ([Mechanics](mechanics.md)) |
| 4 | Manager | Dashboard, assignment, exceptions, quality inspection ([Workshop manager](manager.md)) |
| 5 | Parts | Reserve/issue/return, deliveries, adjustments ([Parts and purchasing](parts-and-purchasing.md)) |
| 6 | Accounts | Invoices, payments, deposits, credit notes, reports ([Invoices and payments](accounts.md)) |

End session 2–6 by running one complete training job from receiving to handover.

## Week 1: parallel running

- All new vehicles are received in WorkshopPro. Keep the existing paper or spreadsheet
  process for invoicing in parallel.
- Daily 10-minute check with the manager: anything confusing, missing or slow. Log each
  issue with time and screen.
- End of week: compare jobs, quotations and payments against the existing records.

## Week 2: WorkshopPro only

- Invoices and payments recorded only in WorkshopPro (if the week-1 comparison matched).
- First restore drill and backup check with the installer.
- End of week: review with the owner — what to fix, what to train again, and the
  decision to continue.

## Fallback

If WorkshopPro is unavailable during the pilot, continue on paper job cards and receipt
books, numbered so they can be entered afterwards. Report the outage to support with the
time it started.

## Measures for the pilot review

- Share of jobs received in WorkshopPro.
- Quotations approved through the link vs recorded by staff.
- Time from receiving to ready for collection (Reports → Operations).
- Invoices issued vs payments recorded; outstanding balance matches the workshop's view.
- Issues reported, fixed, and open.
